Clipfarm privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

What Clipfarm is

Clipfarm is a personal tool run by its owner (LxrdVirus) on their own computer. It creates short highlight videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel.

YouTube API Services

Clipfarm uses YouTube API Services. By using Clipfarm you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data Clipfarm accesses

Clipfarm only asks for permission to upload videos (the youtube.upload scope). It does not read, collect or store your YouTube videos, comments, subscribers, viewing history or any other account data.

How data is stored

The sign-in token Google gives Clipfarm is saved only on the owner's own computer. It is never sent anywhere except to Google to upload videos.

Sharing

Clipfarm does not share, sell or transfer any user data to anyone.

Removing access

You can remove Clipfarm's access at any time on the Google security settings page. Deleting the token.json file on the computer also removes the stored sign-in.
