Clipfarm

Clipfarm is a small personal tool made by LxrdVirus. It turns GTA 6 stream highlights into YouTube Shorts and uploads them to my own YouTube channel.

It only uses footage from creators who have given permission. Every clip credits the original creator and links to their channel.

Clipfarm uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to my own channel. It is not offered to other users.
